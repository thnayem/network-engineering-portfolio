# Connectivity, NTTN, and Data Center Operations

A good part of network work happens away from the device itself. Multi-site links need coordination with providers, fiber faults need follow-up, and DC/DR connectivity needs to be checked before and after a change. I have been involved in this work alongside remote router and switch configuration, maintenance and troubleshooting.

The notes here cover the parts I can explain publicly: OSPF over GRE, multi-site connectivity over NTTN, provider coordination, organization-owned fiber and DC/DR connectivity. I have left out provider names, circuit IDs, real site details, IP addresses and private topology.

## Hands-on production connectivity operations

My work in this area has included remote configuration and maintenance of production routers and switches, routing and tunnel troubleshooting, NTTN/provider follow-up, dark-fiber fault and restoration coordination, and DC/DR connectivity work. For a change, I want the scope and impact understood first, with a backup or baseline, a recovery option and checks after the work is done.
