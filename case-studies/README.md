## Evidence and confidentiality

These case studies describe the scope and method of operational work without identifying employers, providers, customer sites, circuit IDs, real IP addresses, private topology, or confidential service records.

Where measurable results are useful, add only figures that can be verified and are approved for public disclosure. Suitable evidence may include fully redacted monitoring graphs, change records, sanitized command output, topology diagrams using documentation-only addresses, and post-change validation summaries. Never publish raw configurations, credentials, customer data, provider tickets, contracts, or internal screenshots without authorization.

The case studies distinguish confirmed operational responsibilities from example workflows. They do not claim unverified uptime improvements, MTTR reductions, bandwidth gains, or project savings.
